# HERE

**Someone HERE can help.**

---

## What is HERE?

HERE is a location-aware, real-time human connection and help platform.

There are people around us who may have the knowledge, experience, advice, connection, or willingness to help us — but we usually don't know who is approachable. HERE creates a digital layer of **permission and approachability** over the real world.

Users can indicate that they are currently **Reachable**, meaning they are open to receiving questions or requests from other people around them. Someone who needs help, advice, information, recommendations, conversation, or human connection can open HERE and discover people nearby who have explicitly indicated that they are willing to be approached.

> **Someone HERE can help.**

---

## The Problem

People frequently need small amounts of human help or information in everyday situations:

- Someone moves to a new city and doesn't understand how things work
- Someone wants to know which neighbourhood is good for renting
- A student doesn't understand a college process
- Someone wants a local restaurant recommendation
- A traveller needs advice from someone who actually knows the area
- Someone at an airport needs local information
- Someone at an event doesn't know where something is
- Someone wants advice about a profession or skill
- Someone wants to meet people with similar interests
- Someone is new to a gym and wants help using equipment
- Someone simply wants a conversation or wants to make friends
- Someone notices another person they'd like to meet but isn't comfortable approaching them directly

Google, Reddit, and ChatGPT can provide information. But sometimes what a person really needs is **a human who understands the situation and is willing to talk.**

There are potentially dozens or hundreds of such people around them. The problem is the user doesn't know:

- Who should I ask?
- Will they respond?
- Will I disturb them?
- Are they comfortable being approached?
- Will asking feel awkward?

HERE removes that uncertainty.

---

## Origin & Evolution

HERE originally started as a real-time social/dating discovery concept: someone sees another person they find interesting in a café, bar, club, university, or event, but doesn't approach because they don't know whether that person is single, interested, or comfortable being approached. Users would activate **🟢 I'm HERE**, others nearby could discover them, send a **👋 Say Hi**, and only mutual interest (**👋 Hi Back**) would unlock communication.

The concept expanded after realizing the same psychological problem exists far beyond dating — people hesitate to approach strangers not only romantically, but whenever they need help, advice, information, recommendations, local knowledge, guidance, conversation, friendship, professional knowledge, or general social interaction.

HERE evolved from **a permission layer for approaching someone** into **a permission layer for human interaction.** The original social/dating functionality doesn't disappear — it becomes one intent/category within the larger HERE network.

## Core Concept: Reachable

The central status on HERE is:

## 🟢 REACHABLE

A Reachable user is saying: **"I'm available right now, and I'm comfortable with people approaching me about the things I've selected."**

This is one of HERE's most important differentiators — the platform never assumes every nearby user wants interaction. Being Reachable is always voluntary.

### Reachability Preferences

When activating Reachable mode, users specify what they're comfortable being contacted about:

- ☑ Local questions
- ☑ Recommendations
- ☑ Travel
- ☑ Technology
- ☑ Professional advice
- ☑ Study
- ☑ Hobbies
- ☑ Helping someone
- ☑ Conversation
- ☑ Making friends
- ☐ Dating / Meeting someone

Users have granular control. Someone may be happy to answer questions about Cinema 4D but not interested in social requests. Someone else may be open to meeting people but not professional questions.

### Example Profile

**Rohan · 27**
🟢 Reachable Now

**Can help with:** 🏙️ Delhi NCR · 💻 Technology · 🏍️ Motorcycles · 🎮 Gaming · 🏠 Moving to the city
**Languages:** Hindi · English
**Reputation:** 🤝 Helped 32 people · 👍 Highly Helpful

**[ PING ]**

The emphasis is on a person's usefulness, knowledge, interests, and availability — not followers, popularity, or social status.

## Core Interaction Model

HERE revolves around three actions:

**ASK** — I need something.
**HELP** — I can help someone.
**CONNECT** — Let's continue talking.

**ASK ↔ HELP ↔ CONNECT**

### Person-First Discovery

A user opens HERE and sees:

**PEOPLE HERE** — 64 people nearby, 🟢 17 Reachable

They browse Reachable people by distance, knowledge, interests, language, help category, intent, reputation, or relevance. They tap **PING** on a profile, and before sending, HERE asks what they need:

💬 Ask something · 🙋 Need help · 🧭 Local advice · 👋 Say Hi · ☕ Meet / Hangout · ➕ Something else

The recipient sees what the person wants *before* accepting, and chooses **I CAN HELP** or **CAN'T RIGHT NOW**. If accepted, chat opens.

### Question-First Discovery (Ask HERE)

Users don't always have to manually browse profiles. They can broadcast a request:

**ASK HERE**
> "Just moved to Noida. Which areas near Sector 62 are good for renting?"

HERE intelligently distributes the request to relevant Reachable people nearby based on location, expertise, interests, language, and past helpfulness. Someone who knows the answer taps **I CAN HELP**, and the requester decides whether to continue the conversation.

### Help Mode

Reachable users can also browse from the other direction:

**PEOPLE NEEDING HELP** — 7 people around you need help

> "Does anyone know which metro gate goes toward Sector 62?"
> "Any good vegetarian cafés nearby?"
> "I'm new to Cinema 4D. Anyone here experienced with Octane?"

Reachable users tap **I KNOW THIS** / **I CAN HELP**. HERE is two-sided — people don't only consume help, they contribute it.

## Reputation

HERE rewards helpful behaviour without becoming a popularity contest. Potential signals: 🤝 People Helped, 👍 Helpful ratings, ⭐ Trusted Helper, 🏙️ Local Guide, 💻 Technology Helper, 🎓 Study Helper.

HERE deliberately avoids follower counts, like counts, influencer-style metrics, and public popularity contests. Status on HERE comes from **being useful**, not being famous.

---

## Location Model

Location provides context, not surveillance. Exact GPS positioning is never unnecessarily exposed.

| Level | Range | Useful for |
|---|---|---|
| 📍 HERE | Same venue / immediate area | Events, airports, universities, cafés, offices, conferences |
| 🚶 AROUND | ~1–2 km | Recommendations, small help, local questions |
| 🏙️ NEARBY | ~5–10 km | Services, neighbourhood knowledge |
| 🌆 CITY | City-wide | Moving, housing, commuting, jobs, general city knowledge |

HERE never shows interfaces like *"Priya is exactly 17 metres away."* Instead: *"Priya is nearby."*

## Privacy & Safety

Because HERE combines strangers with real-world proximity, safety is fundamental:

- **Reachable is opt-in** — users never automatically appear as approachable
- **Temporary availability** — Reachable status expires (30 min / 1 hr / 2 hrs / until turned off)
- **No exact location** — approximate proximity or venue-based presence only
- **Request preview** — recipients see what someone wants before accepting communication
- **Mutual communication** — a Ping never automatically unlocks unlimited messaging
- **Spam prevention** — excessive Pings are limited
- **Rejection protection** — if someone declines, the sender cannot repeatedly recontact them
- **Block/report** — accessible everywhere
- **Verification** — phone, email, selfie, and optional identity verification
- **Age protection** — dating/social functionality carries appropriate age restrictions and controls

## Social & Dating Functionality

The original HERE concept exists inside the larger platform as an optional intent:

### ❤️ Open to Meeting

Only people with mutually compatible preferences see or send these requests. Someone who hasn't enabled this intent never receives romantic requests simply because they're Reachable for another category — this separation is intentional and important.

Potential intents: 🤝 Help · 💬 Conversation · 👫 Friendship · ☕ Hangout · 💼 Professional networking · ❤️ Dating

HERE supports human connection without branding itself as a dating app.

---

## Product Principles

HERE should never become Instagram, Facebook, Reddit, Tinder, LinkedIn, Urban Company, and Google Maps all combined into one application.

Avoid unnecessary: reels, stories, followers, influencers, engagement bait, endless feeds, businesses/marketplace features (early on), gig-work/payment mechanics (initially), and endless city-wide profile swiping.

Every feature must answer: **Does this make it easier for one human to comfortably approach another human who is willing to interact?** If not, question whether HERE needs it.

## Core User Flows

**Person-first:** Open HERE → Activate Reachable → See Reachable people → Filter by need/interest → Open profile → Ping → Explain what you need → Recipient accepts/declines → Chat → Help occurs → Rate the interaction

**Question-first:** Open HERE → Ask HERE → Write question → HERE identifies relevant Reachable users → Someone taps "I Can Help" → Requester views profile → Accept → Chat → Problem solved → Reputation increases

**Helper flow:** Open HERE → Activate Reachable → See "People need help" → Browse relevant questions → Tap "I Can Help" → Requester accepts → Chat → Help → Reputation increases

## Competitive Landscape

- **IamHere** — Indian hyperlocal social network for discovering nearby people, professionals, businesses, NGOs, and communities
- **ZetPin** — Indian hyperlocal platform with nearby Q&A, local services, chat/calls, and helper karma — one of the closest conceptual competitors
- **PadosXO** — Indian neighbourhood/community platform, particularly relevant in Delhi NCR
- **Anybody** — nearby request/help platform where users broadcast a request and nearby people respond — very similar to the ASK → HELP interaction
- **Ask Around** — nearby help/request system with optional paid assistance
- **Jodel** — hyperlocal anonymous discussion/community
- **Nextdoor** — neighbourhood community/recommendation network
- **Couchsurfing Hangouts** — nearby people explicitly indicating availability to socialize
- **Happn and similar dating apps** — overlap with HERE's original proximity-based social concept

### Differentiation

HERE is not simply "an app for asking nearby people questions" — that space already exists. HERE's core product object is not the post. It's:

# THE REACHABLE PERSON

Someone explicitly communicating: **"I'm available, and you can approach me about these things."**

This combines human expertise, real-world proximity, explicit availability, permission to approach, questions/requests, and human connection — with the psychological reduction of **approach anxiety** at the center of the product.

## Launch Strategy

HERE depends heavily on network density. The biggest failure state is **0 Reachable people around you.** The initial launch should not attempt nationwide coverage — it should start hyperlocally (Noida, Greater Noida, Delhi NCR, universities, corporate campuses, coworking spaces, selected cafés, tech parks, conferences, festivals).

**5,000 highly concentrated users** beats **100,000 geographically scattered users.**

Key metrics: daily Reachable users, Reachable density per location, questions asked, response rate, median response time, successful connections, helpful interactions, repeat helpers, repeat requesters, percentage of users who both ask AND help, safety/report rate, retention.

## The Core Hypothesis

The important question is not *"will people ask questions?"* — people already do that everywhere.

The critical hypothesis is:

> **Will enough people voluntarily make themselves Reachable and respond to nearby strangers?**

If yes, HERE becomes a live directory of humans who have explicitly said: **"You can approach me."**
